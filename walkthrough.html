Feature walkthrough

Everything the family dashboard can do, where to find it, and the shortest path to getting useful information into it.

▦ Open FeaturesTap Features in the bottom toolbar to see every available module, even when it has no toolbar shortcut.
＋ Add a shortcutInside Features, tap Add beside anything you use often. Tap On toolbar to remove it later.
⚙️ Family availabilitySettings controls which modules the whole family can use. Toolbar shortcuts belong only to the signed-in person on that device.
🔔 Your notificationsOpen Settings → Notifications to turn all alerts on or off, then choose posts, photos, calendar, care, money, or other activity. Your own actions never alert you.
🔒 Protect secretsUse safe references and private links. Never enter passwords, PINs, full account numbers, card numbers, or identity numbers.

Changes are shared with the family unless the feature says otherwise. Records show who added them, and most can be edited by any signed-in family member while only their creator can delete them. Privacy Policy · Terms
