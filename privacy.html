Privacy Policy

Effective August 6, 2026

Family Dashboard is a private, invitation-only application for sharing information within one family. It has no advertising, analytics, or data brokerage.

Information used by the dashboard

  • Google account information: your name, email address, profile picture, and account identifier are used to sign you in and identify your contributions.
  • Family information: information family members choose to add, including posts, calendar entries, household records, care information, financial reference records, and feature preferences.
  • Google Drive content: when you approve Drive access, the browser reads or changes files in the family’s shared folder for photo, video, document, and upload features.
  • Invitation email permission: Gmail send permission is requested only when a signed-in member explicitly chooses to email an invitation.
  • Device information: settings, cached thumbnails, and sign-in state may be saved on the device so the app opens quickly and does not require a login every time.

Where information is stored

Shared dashboard records are stored in the family’s Firebase project. Media and documents remain in the family’s Google Drive folder. Google access tokens are short-lived and are used directly by the browser; the application does not ask for or store Google passwords.

Who can see family information

Signing in is not enough to enter the dashboard. A person must also have a valid family invitation or already be on the family member list. Family members can see shared family records. Drive files are additionally controlled by the sharing permissions on the family’s Drive folder.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the dashboard’s family-sharing features, keep records attributable to the person who added or updated them, operate invitations, and maintain the signed-in session. Information is not sold or used for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

Shared records remain until a permitted family member deletes them or the family administrator removes them from Firebase or Drive. Signing out removes the active session from that device. Clearing the browser’s site data removes locally saved configuration and caches. A Google permission grant can also be revoked from the Google Account connections page.

Security and sensitive information

The dashboard uses Google and Firebase access controls, but no application can guarantee perfect security. Do not enter passwords, Social Security numbers, complete bank or card numbers, authentication codes, or other credentials. Medical and financial areas are family reference tools, not professional systems.

Questions or deletion requests

Contact the family member who sent your invitation or the administrator of your family’s dashboard. That person controls the family member list, Firebase data, and shared Drive folder.
